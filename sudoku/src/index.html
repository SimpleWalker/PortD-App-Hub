How to play

Fill the grid so every row, column and 3×3 box contains 1–9 once.
Click a cell, then a number. Keyboard: 1–9 enter, arrows move, Delete erases, N toggles notes, H gives a hint, Ctrl+Z undoes, Space pauses.
In notes mode, numbers are pencilled in; placing a number clears it from the notes of related cells.
