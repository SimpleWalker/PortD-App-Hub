How to play

Reveal every square that isn't a mine. Numbers show how many mines touch that square.

Left-click reveal · Right-click flag / question mark · Click a number whose flags are all placed to reveal its neighbours.
Touch: long-press to flag, or switch on Flag mode.
F2 or the face starts a new game. Your first click is always safe.

Keyboard: arrow keys move, Space or Enter reveals (on a number: its neighbours), F flags.
