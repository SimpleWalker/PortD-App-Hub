Freeze Test

This program makes itself stop responding, so that you can see what PortD does with a frozen program. It changes nothing on your drive.

Answering

What to expect

  • The clock above stops while the program is frozen.
  • Other programs stop too. The browser runs all programs together, apart from PortD, so open programs freeze with this one. PortD itself and its built-in apps, such as Notepad, keep working.
  • PortD 1.1 and later marks frozen programs after about 6 seconds: a red title bar saying (Not responding), and a red bar at the top with Close all programs.
  • PortD 1.0 shows nothing. Close this window with its × button; if other programs stay frozen, close them too.
  • After Freeze for 15 seconds, everything answers again by itself.
