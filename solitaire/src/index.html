How to play

Build each foundation (top right) up by suit from Ace to King. In the seven columns, stack cards down in alternating colours; only a King can fill an empty column.
Drag cards to move them, or click/tap a card to send it to the best place. Click the stock to turn cards. Ctrl+Z undo, F2 new game, Space turns the stock.
Keyboard: arrow keys move between piles and cards, Enter plays the card like a click, M picks cards up and M again puts them down on another pile, A auto-finishes.
