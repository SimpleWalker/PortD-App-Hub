    Keyboard shortcuts

    TabMove between columns, cards and buttons
    ↑ ↓ ← →Go to the next card up, down, or in the next column
    Ctrl+↑ Ctrl+↓Move the card up or down in its column
    Ctrl+← Ctrl+→Move the card to the next column
    Enter or SpaceOpen the card
    M or Shift+F10Card menu: Move to, top, bottom, archive
    DeleteArchive the card (restore it from Archive)
    Alt+NNew card (in the column you are in)
    Ctrl+FSearch
    Alt+AArchived cards
    EscClose a dialog or menu, or stop a drag
